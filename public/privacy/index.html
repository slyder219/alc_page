ALC FF Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · A Leading Concept LLC

This policy covers the ALC FF app for iPhone and the FF web app at app.aleadingconcept.com (together, "FF"). FF is a tool freight forwarders use to run their shipments, documents and accounts. "We" means A Leading Concept LLC.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, password and the business you belong to. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Business records: what you and your team enter or upload, such as shipments, the names and addresses of shippers and consignees, air waybills, documents, rates, invoices and payments.
  • Server logs: basic request records, such as IP address and the page requested, used to keep the service running and secure.

The app stores your sign-in on your device in the iOS Keychain. It doesn't collect your location, contacts or photos, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. FF has no advertising and no third-party analytics.

How we use it

Only to run FF: to sign you in, show each business its own records, and produce the documents and accounts you ask for. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

We use a small number of service providers, and they handle data only on our behalf:

  • Google Cloud: hosting for the FF servers
  • Neon: the database
  • Google Drive: storage for shipment documents
  • Anthropic: reading a document you upload to fill in a form, only where a business has this feature on
  • SkyLink and CargoAI: flight searches, only where a business has these features on

We share data with anyone else only if the law requires it.

Retention and deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time. Sign in on the web at app.aleadingconcept.com, open Account settings and choose Delete account, or email us and we'll do it for you. Your sign-in and personal details are removed.

Shipments and accounting records belong to the business, not to one login, so they stay with the business after a person leaves. If you are the last person in a business, deleting your account deletes the business and all of its data. An owner with other people still in the business hands ownership on first.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each business can see only its own records, and access inside a business is limited by role.

Children

FF is a business tool and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or requests: hello@aleadingconcept.com.
