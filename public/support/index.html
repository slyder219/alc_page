ALC FF Support

ALC FF is the iPhone app for the FF freight forwarding workspace. For help with the app or your account, email hello@aleadingconcept.com.

Signing in

Use the same username and password as the web app at app.aleadingconcept.com. The app is for staff accounts. If you've forgotten your password, email us.

If a shipment looks wrong or is missing

Pull down on the list to refresh. You see only your own business's shipments, and prices appear only for roles that include them.

Deleting your account

Sign in on the web, open Account settings and choose Delete account, or email us and we'll do it for you. The privacy policy explains what is removed.
